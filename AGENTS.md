# Repository working constraints

This file contains repository-specific product, legal, administrative, and deployment boundaries. Keep assistant-, model-, skill-, and orchestration-specific policy outside this file.

- Keep this a static, root-deployed company/studio site. Preserve company voice, current product identities, and substantive copy. Do not add a framework, build step, or runtime dependency without explicit authorization.
- Preserve relative public links, `CNAME`, `.nojekyll`, `impressum.html`, and especially `privacy.html`; its root URL is used by the browser-extension store listing. Do not change legal text, privacy commitments, contact details, or external/security boundaries as incidental cleanup.
- Card content in `index.html` supplies the dialog; do not maintain a second product catalog. Preserve native dialog accessibility, keyboard activation, focus restoration, and responsive behavior.
- Use `docs/media.md` for imagery work, `docs/deployment.md` for domain/hosting work, and `docs/administration.md` for established expense bookkeeping. Do not fabricate original product media or infer live DNS from historical records.
- Source and fresh browser evidence establish implementation behavior. Tests do not authorize changes to product intent, legal commitments, deployment, or external services.
- Preserve supplied source documents and administrative evidence unless directed otherwise. Keep temporary work out of deliverables.
