Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This policy explains how Crazy Entertainment Marmullaku ("Crazy Entertainment", "we", "us") handles data on this website and in Crazy Enhancer for YouTube. Other products presented on this site have their own privacy notices where applicable. The short version for the services covered here: we collect no personal data. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data, and we run no advertising or behavioural tracking.

This website

This site (crazyentertainment.ch) is a static website. We set no cookies, run no analytics, and use no tracking of any kind.

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). As the host, GitHub may process standard technical information such as your IP address in server logs for delivery and security. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts: the page loads web fonts from Google Fonts; when it loads, your browser requests the font files from Google, which may receive your IP address as part of that request. We use this only to render the typeface — for nothing else.
  • Links & email: if you email us, we receive what you send us, and use it only to reply.

Crazy Enhancer for YouTube (browser extension)

Our browser extension collects no data whatsoever. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, browsing history, or usage analytics. There are no trackers and no third-party servers.

  • What is stored, and where: your settings (chosen playback speed, button order, enabled features, and any "Open with" sites you configure) are stored locally in your own browser using the browser's storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.
  • Permissions: storage — to save your settings and chosen speed locally so they persist; clipboardWrite — to copy a video's transcript to your clipboard when you click the Transcript button (the transcript is processed locally and is never sent anywhere); access to youtube.com — the extension runs only on YouTube, adds its controls to YouTube pages, and reads the page's own caption data locally to produce the transcript. It does not access any other website.
  • The "Open with" feature: this optional button opens the current video's link, in a new tab, on a site you have configured yourself. The extension only opens the link; it sends your data nowhere. No sites are included by default.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU GDPR you have rights regarding personal data we hold about you. Because we are designed not to collect personal data, there is in practice little to access, correct, or erase — but you are welcome to contact us with any question or request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it.
